# Thai Food Guide

A one-page website that introduces Thai dishes to visitors and helps them decide what to eat.

## Language

**Dish**:
A single Thai food or dessert item shown on the page, such as Pad Thai or Mango Sticky Rice. In Thai copy it is always called "เมนู".
_Avoid_: Menu item, recipe, food; in Thai copy, "จาน"

**Today's Pick**:
A Dish chosen at random for the visitor to consider eating today. The visitor can ask for a new one any number of times, and it is never the same Dish as the previous pick.
_Avoid_: Daily menu, dish of the day

**Favorite**:
A Dish the visitor has marked as liked. Favorites are kept on the visitor's own device and can be viewed together as a group. Today's Pick is always drawn from all Dishes, not only Favorites.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, wishlist, liked dish
